How it works

1

Enter details

Passenger profile, flight information and 14 service ratings on a 0–5 scale.

2

Get a prediction

The model returns a label and the probability that the passenger is satisfied.

3

See why

SHAP values show which inputs pushed the prediction toward or away from "Satisfied".

Please note: satisfaction is measured in a post-flight survey and several inputs are themselves satisfaction ratings, so this tool classifies overall satisfaction from component ratings — it does not forecast satisfaction before a flight. Feature contributions describe the model's behaviour, not causes.
